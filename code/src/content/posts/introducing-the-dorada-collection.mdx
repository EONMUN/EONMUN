## A New Chapter in Gold

The **Dorada Collection** represents a deep exploration of gold leaf techniques combined with traditional watercolor methods. Each piece in this collection captures the interplay between precious metallic surfaces and organic, flowing pigments.

### The Inspiration

Working with gold leaf has always fascinated me. The way light catches the surface at different angles transforms each piece throughout the day. *Persimmons del Oro* and *Limones del Cobre* are two key works that embody this vision.

### Techniques Used

- Traditional gold leaf application on watercolor paper
- Layered watercolor washes beneath metallic surfaces
- Pencil detailing for organic forms

I hope you enjoy exploring this collection as much as I enjoyed creating it.
