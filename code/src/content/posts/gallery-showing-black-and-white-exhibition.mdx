## Upcoming Exhibition

I'm excited to announce that several pieces from the **B&W collection** will be featured in an upcoming group exhibition focusing on monochromatic works.

### Exhibition Details

- **Venue**: Community Arts Gallery
- **Dates**: October 15 - November 30, 2025
- **Opening Reception**: October 15, 6-9 PM

### Featured Works

*Quiescent Citadel* will be the centerpiece of my contribution, alongside several newer works from the collection. The exhibition explores how artists use the absence of color to create depth and emotion.

### Visit

The gallery is open Tuesday through Saturday, 11 AM to 6 PM. I'll be present at the opening reception and would love to meet collectors and fellow art enthusiasts.

More details to follow as the date approaches.
