## Gold Leaf for Beginners

Gold leaf is one of the most rewarding materials to work with, but it can be intimidating at first. Here are some techniques I've developed over years of practice.

### Materials You'll Need

1. **Gold leaf sheets** - I recommend starting with imitation gold leaf before moving to genuine
2. **Sizing adhesive** - Water-based sizing is easier to control
3. **Soft brushes** - A gilder's tip and a soft mop brush
4. **Burnishing tool** - An agate burnisher works best

### Step-by-Step Process

First, prepare your surface. The paper or canvas must be completely dry and free of dust. Apply a thin, even coat of sizing and wait until it reaches the right tack.

> The key to successful gold leaf application is patience. Rush the sizing and you'll get wrinkles. Wait too long and it won't adhere.

### Common Mistakes

- Applying leaf to wet sizing
- Using too much adhesive
- Not sealing the finished surface

Practice on scrap paper before committing to a final piece. The results are worth the effort.
